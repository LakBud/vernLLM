title: Features Overview
description: The traces, metrics, and content capture vern-llm-otel produces
---

`vern-llm-otel` produces three kinds of telemetry from the same middleware. They share one set of
events, so they always agree with each other, and each can be tuned on its own.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Traces" href="/docs/integrations/otel/features/traces">
    A call span per call and an attempt span per attempt, with every retry and fallback as events.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Metrics" href="/docs/integrations/otel/features/metrics">
    The GenAI client metrics, plus counters and histograms for retries, fallbacks, and rate limits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Content Capture" href="/docs/integrations/otel/features/content-capture">
    Opt in prompt and response capture, with redaction, truncation, and per call gating.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## Which one answers what

| Question                                                          | Look at                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What happened to this one call, step by step?                     | [Traces](/docs/integrations/otel/features/traces)                   |
| How often do calls retry or fall back, and how long do they take? | [Metrics](/docs/integrations/otel/features/metrics)                 |
| What did the model actually receive and answer?                   | [Content Capture](/docs/integrations/otel/features/content-capture) |

Traces and metrics are on by default. Content capture is off until you turn it on.
